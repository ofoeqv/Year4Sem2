## Question 1
A simple p+-n junction is designed to work as an IMPATT diode.

The doping concentration in the p+ layer is 10^19 cm^-3, while the doping in the n-layer is 0.7 x 10^16 cm^-3.

Calculate the peak electric field if the breakdown voltage is 80 V and the dielectric constant is 11.9. Express your answer in kV/cm. Round your answer to 2 decimal places.

Answer: 412.50 kV/cm **[correct]**

Accepted range: 412.50 ± 5%

## Question 2
A basic IMPATT diode was designed with a total depletion width of 3.7 um and an injection region width of xa, such that an operation frequency of 44 GHz is achieved.

Assuming the saturated carrier velocity vs = 1.66 x 10^5 m s^-1, what value of xa is required to achieve this operating frequency?

Express your answer in um. Round your answer to 2 decimal places.

Answer: 1.81 um **[correct]**

Accepted range: 1.81 ± 5%

## Question 3
A simple Gunn diode has a lightly doped n- layer sandwiched between two highly doped n+ layers.

The drift velocity is 0.5 x 10^5 m s^-1. What is the transit time frequency if the n- layer is 2.48 um thick?

Express your answer in GHz. Round your answer to 1 decimal place.

Answer: 20.2 GHz **[correct]**

Accepted range: 20.2 ± 5%

## Question 4
A quantum cascade laser can be used to generate a wave at 3.86 THz.

What is the required energy gap in the active quantum well of the cascade laser? Express your answer in meV. Round your answer to 2 decimal places.
