## Question 1
Al_xGa_1-xAs is very close to lattice matched to GaAs and is often used to create a heterostructure in GaAs-based lasers. Calculate the Al_xGa_1-xAs bandgap if x = 0.16, using the formula:

E_g(x) = 1.43 + 1.707x - 1.437x^2 + 1.31x^3

Round your answer to 2 decimal places.

Answer: 1.67 eV **[correct]**

Accepted range: 1.67 ± 0.1
