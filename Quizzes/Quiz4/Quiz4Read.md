## Question 1
Consider a Si pn junction with the following parameters:

- Diode area, A = 1 mm^2
- Electron diffusion coefficient, De = 22.5 cm^2/s
- Hole diffusion coefficient, Dh = 10.8 cm^2/s
- Electron minority carrier lifetime, tau_e = 96 ns
- Hole minority carrier lifetime, tau_h = 11 ns
- Intrinsic carrier concentration, ni = 9.65 x 10^9 cm^-3

Calculate the minority electron diffusion length. Express your answer in micrometres. Round your answer to 2 decimal places.

Answer: 14.69 um **[correct]**

Accepted range: 14.69 ± 10%

## Question 2
A GaAs pn junction solar cell has the following parameters:

- Built-in voltage = 1 V
- Diode area, A = 1 mm^2
- p-side doping, NA = 0.4704 x 10^16 cm^-3
- n-side doping, ND = 26.5 x 10^16 cm^-3
- Intrinsic carrier concentration, ni = 2.26 x 10^6 cm^-3
- Dielectric constant = 12.9

Calculate the depletion width of this solar cell. Express your answer in micrometres. Round your answer to 2 decimal places.

Answer: 0.56 um **[correct]**

Accepted range: 0.56 ± 10%

## Question 3
Consider a GaAs pn junction solar cell with:

- Depletion width, W = 0.5 um
- Minority electron diffusion length, Le = 20.9 x 10^-6 m
- Minority hole diffusion length, Lh = 8.8 x 10^-6 m
- Required photocurrent = 0.046 A
- Carrier generation rate = 7.1 x 10^22 cm^-3 s^-1

What is the required solar cell area to generate the photocurrent? Express your answer in m^2. Round your answer to 2 decimal places.

Answer: 1.34E-7 m^2 **[correct]**

Accepted range: 1.34E-7 ± 10%

## Question 4

Calculate the required value of saturation current to achieve an open circuit voltage of 0.6 V at room temperature. Express your answer in A. Round your answer to 2 decimal places.

Answer: 9.12E-12 A **[correct]**

## Question 7
Which of the following two statements are true?

A. The maximum detection wavelength of a solar cell is longer if the bandgap is smaller. **[correct]**
B. A solar cell generates a larger open circuit voltage if the semiconductor bandgap is larger. **[correct]**
C. More electron-hole pairs are generated for each absorbed photon when the photon energy is larger.
D. A solar cell with a smaller bandgap produces a larger open circuit voltage.

Feedback: Only one electron-hole pair can be produced per absorbed photon. A larger bandgap material has lower dark current and hence can produce a larger open circuit voltage Voc.

## Question 8
Select two factors that can increase the generation of photocurrent in a solar cell.

A. Introduce surface engineering on the solar cell to reduce light reflection. **[correct]**
B. Terminate dangling bonds at the device surface. **[correct]**
C. Increase the bandgap of the semiconductor.
D. Use a semiconductor material with a small minority carrier lifetime.

Feedback: Increasing the bandgap limits the wavelength range that can be absorbed and, in general, a wider bandgap has lower absorption coefficient at a given wavelength. The carrier recombination rate is higher if the minority carrier lifetime is small, so the photocurrent will be lower.
