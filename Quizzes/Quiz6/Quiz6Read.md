## Question 5
Select all the advantages of a quantum well LED over the p-n homojunction LED.

A. The emission wavelength of the quantum well can be controlled by changing the bandgap of the wide bandgap barrier layer.
B. The quantum well produces higher output power. **[correct]**
C. A single quantum well design produces a wider emission spectrum.
D. The quantum well LED has a lower threshold current. **[correct]**
E. The quantum well has a lower electron density than the pn junction at a given energy.

Feedback: The emission wavelength is determined by the bandgap of the active region, not the barrier. A single quantum well produces a narrower spectrum. The density of states is higher in the quantum well.

## Question 6
Which of the following statements are true? Select all correct answers.

A. Adding P to GaAs reduces the emission wavelength. **[correct]**
B. Increasing the P composition in GaxIn1-xAs1-yPy reduces the emission wavelength. **[correct]**
C. A GaxIn1-xN semiconductor with a lower x composition emits a shorter wavelength.
D. A GaxIn1-xAs quantum well layer produces a longer wavelength with a higher x value.
E. Si LEDs are the most widely used LEDs in the visible wavelength range.

Feedback: Increasing x in GaInAs increases the bandgap and reduces the emission wavelength. Silicon is an indirect bandgap semiconductor and does not produce significant emission, so it is not used as LEDs. Reducing x in GaInN reduces the bandgap, so the emission wavelength is longer.

## Question 7
A near infrared LED emitting light at a wavelength of 940 nm is required for a LIDAR system.

Which design would you recommend?

A. In0.15Ga0.85As pn homojunction grown on a GaAs substrate.
B. In0.15Ga0.85As / Al0.2Ga0.8As quantum well grown on a GaAs substrate. **[correct]**
C. GaAs / Al0.2Ga0.8As quantum well grown on a GaAs substrate.
D. Al0.2Ga0.8As / GaAs / Al0.2Ga0.8As double heterojunction.
E. GaAs pn homojunction grown on a GaAs substrate.

Feedback: Only In0.15Ga0.85As has a small enough bandgap for the required emission wavelength. Since it is not lattice matched to the GaAs substrate, the pn homojunction option is not suitable.
