# Quiz 1 - Semiconductor properties

Source: Quiz1answerswhole.pdf

## Question 1
Semiconductors typically have bandgap values of

A. Between 100 meV to 5 eV **[correct]**
B. Below 10 meV
C. Between 6 to 10 eV
D. Above 10 eV

Feedback: Indeed most semiconductors have bandgaps between 100 meV and 5 eV. Semiconductors with bandgaps below 10 meV may be considered semimetal while those with bandgaps above 6 eV may be considered insulator.
