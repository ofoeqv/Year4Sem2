# Question 10 - Digital System Advantages and Aliasing

## Category
Theory

## Topic
Advantages of digital signal processing

## Question
Which of the following statements incorrectly describes an advantage of a digital system, compared to the analogue equivalent?

## Options
A. Flexibility: Data can be sampled in real-time, and if necessary, readily processed off-line at some later date.

B. Repeatability: Since digital systems are configured by software or external digital hardware, the functions that they perform are very repeatable.

C. Stability: Storage and processing of data by digital hardware means that problems associated with analogue processing, i.e. component value variations due to changes in environment temperature and ageing, are not encountered.

D. Avoiding aliasing: Digital systems do not result in aliasing that can result in unexpected and disruptive distortions of the system output.

## Correct Answer
D. Avoiding aliasing

## Explanation
A, B and C are genuine advantages of digital systems: flexibility, repeatability and stability/reduced sensitivity to analogue component variation.

D is incorrect because digital systems can suffer from aliasing if the input signal is not sampled fast enough or if proper anti-alias filtering is not used before sampling. Aliasing is specifically a sampling-related problem, so digital systems do not automatically avoid it.

## Must-know concept
Digital systems have many advantages over analogue systems, but aliasing is not automatically avoided. To avoid aliasing, the sampling frequency must be high enough and the input should be bandlimited before sampling.
