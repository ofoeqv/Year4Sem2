# Question 12 - Advantages of Digital Implementation

## Category
Theory

## Topic
Digital signal processing advantages

## Question
Which of the following is **not** an advantage of a digital implementation of a system, compared to the analogue equivalent?

## Options
A. Data compression: digital sequences can easily be compressed for transmission.

B. Cost: The application of digital signal processors is becoming increasingly widespread, and hence, in many cases, the cost of digital algorithm implementation is less than analogue equivalents.

C. Repeatability: Since digital systems are configured by software or external digital hardware, the functions that they perform are very repeatable.

D. Speed: Sampled systems operate more quickly than their analogue equivalents.

## Correct Answer
**D. Speed: Sampled systems operate more quickly than their analogue equivalents.**

## Explanation
Data compression, cost, and repeatability are recognised advantages of digital signal processing.

However, **speed** is not automatically an advantage of digital systems. A digital/sampled system needs sampling, analogue-to-digital conversion, processing, and sometimes digital-to-analogue conversion. These stages can introduce delay, so a sampled system is not necessarily quicker than an analogue equivalent.

## Must-know concept
Digital systems are often preferred because of flexibility, programmability, repeatability, stability, data compression, and sometimes cost. But they are not automatically faster than analogue systems.
