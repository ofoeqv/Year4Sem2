# Quiz 4 Question 7

## Question
Which of the following two statements are true?

A. The maximum detection wavelength of a solar cell is longer if the bandgap is smaller.
B. A solar cell generates a larger open circuit voltage if the semiconductor bandgap is larger.
C. More electron-hole pairs are generated for each absorbed photon when the photon energy is larger.
D. A solar cell with a smaller bandgap produces a larger open circuit voltage.

## Answer
A and B

## Feedback
Only one electron-hole pair can be produced per absorbed photon.

A larger bandgap material has lower dark current and therefore can produce a larger open circuit voltage Voc.

A smaller bandgap gives a longer maximum detection wavelength because:
