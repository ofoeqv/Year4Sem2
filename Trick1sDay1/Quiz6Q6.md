# Quiz 6 Question 6

## Question
Which of the following statements are true? Select all correct answers.

A. Adding P to GaAs reduces the emission wavelength.
B. Increasing the P composition in GaxIn1-xAs1-yPy reduces the emission wavelength.
C. A GaxIn1-xN semiconductor with a lower x composition emits a shorter wavelength.
D. A GaxIn1-xAs quantum well layer produces a longer wavelength with a higher x value.
E. Si LEDs are the most widely used LEDs in the visible wavelength range.

## Answer
A and B

## Feedback
Adding P to GaAs increases the bandgap, so the emission wavelength decreases.

Increasing the P composition in GaxIn1-xAs1-yPy also increases the bandgap and reduces the emission wavelength.

The following statements are wrong:

- A GaxIn1-xN semiconductor with a lower x composition emits a shorter wavelength. Reducing x reduces the bandgap, so the emission wavelength becomes longer.
- A GaxIn1-xAs quantum well layer produces a longer wavelength with a higher x value. Increasing x increases the bandgap, so the emission wavelength becomes shorter.
- Si LEDs are the most widely used LEDs in the visible wavelength range. Silicon is an indirect bandgap semiconductor and does not produce significant light emission.
