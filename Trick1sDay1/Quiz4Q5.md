# Quiz 4 Question 5

## Question
The absorption coefficient of a semiconductor is 6,840 cm^-1 at a wavelength of 0.68 um.

Calculate the thickness of absorption required to absorb 42% of the incident light.

Express your answer in cm. Round your answer to 2 decimal places.

## Answer
7.96E-5 cm

Accepted range: 7.96E-5 ± 10%
