# Quiz 3 Question 1

## Question
In an n-type semiconductor, Nc is the effective density of states in the conduction band.

Assuming that Nc = 1.1 x 10^19 cm^-3 and an n-type dopant concentration of 24.9 x 10^16 cm^-3 is incorporated into this semiconductor, calculate the room temperature Fermi level.

Provide your answer in eV, relative to the bottom of the conduction band Ec. You may assume complete ionization of the n-type dopant.

Round your answer to 3 decimal places.

## Answer
-0.098 eV

Accepted range: -0.098 ± 10%

## Feedback / formula
Use:

EF = EC + kT[ln(n/Nc) + (1/8)(n/Nc)]

kT = 0.026 eV at room temperature T = 300 K.

The negative answer shows EF is below EC.
