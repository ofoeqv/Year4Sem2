# Quiz 3 Question 8

## Question
Which of the following two statements are true?

A. Increasing dopant concentration will reduce the conductivity of the semiconductor.
B. Increasing the dopant concentration will reduce the resistivity of the semiconductor.
C. Increasing the n-type dopants will increase the Fermi level in the n-type semiconductor.
D. Increasing the n-type dopant concentration will increase the electron mobility.

## Answer
B and C

## Feedback
Increasing the n-type dopants does not increase the electron mobility, because:

mu = q tau / m*

Increasing the n-type dopants will increase the conductivity and therefore reduce the resistivity of the n-type semiconductor.
