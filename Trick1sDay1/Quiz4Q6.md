# Quiz 4 Question 6

## Question
What is the maximum detection wavelength of a Si solar cell?

A. 1127 nm
B. 1550 nm
C. 1.24 um
D. 1.00 um
E. 980 nm

## Answer
A. 1127 nm

## Feedback / formula
Use:

lambda = 1.24 / Eg

For Si, Eg is about 1.1 eV, giving a maximum detection wavelength of about 1.127 um = 1127 nm.
