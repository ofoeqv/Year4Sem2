# Quiz 4 Question 4

## Question
Consider a solar cell made of Al0.3Ga0.7As. When exposed to sunlight, it generates a photocurrent of 0.096 A.

Calculate the required value of saturation current to achieve an open circuit voltage of 0.6 V at room temperature.

Express your answer in A. Round your answer to 2 decimal places.

## Answer
9.12E-12 A

Accepted range: 9.12E-12 ± 15%
